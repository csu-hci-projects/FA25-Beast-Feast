# Game Controls & Objective - Beast Feast

## Objective
You are an alien slime sent to a distant planet with one mission: become the biggest creature in the ecosystem.

Consume other creatures to grow, climb the food chain, and reach the max size of 10 so you can challenge the boss of each level.

Explore with caution! Every creature is a potential meal or a potential threat. Explore the island or dungeon, evolve, and eventually face the mysterious boss. We won’t tell you what it is, but you’ll know when you find it.

## Controls
### Movement
* **W / A / S / D** – Move the slime
### Eating Beasts
* **Space Bar** – Eat a creature when you’re close enough and strong enough.
### Size Indicator
* **Left Shift (Hold)** – Reveal “edibility bubbles” above nearby creatures:
    * **Green** - You can eat the creature, and it *cannot* eat you.
    * **Yellow** – You can eat the creature, but it can also hurt you, costing one hit point.
    * **Red** – You cannot eat the creature, but it can *eat* you.
